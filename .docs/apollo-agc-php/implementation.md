# Implementation: apollo-agc-php

Plan: [`plan.md`](plan.md)

## Changes applied

### 1. Skelet: de maanlander valt zichtbaar op de pagina

Verified by two builders. From the command line: `composer install` succeeded, `vendor/bin/phpunit` passed with 49 tests and 3622 assertions, and `vendor/bin/phpstan analyse` reported no errors at level 8. `curl -si /` returned the CSP from §4 and `nosniff`, and `/api.php` set the cookie `apollo` with `HttpOnly` and `SameSite=Strict`. Seven invalid requests against the running server each returned the expected status with only `{"error":…}`. In Chrome: the page showed "Start landing", the lander fell on the Canvas from 15240 m to 0 m in 172.2 s with the altitude dropping on every tick, and the page showed "Outcome: crashed". Over 130 ticks the peak number of concurrent `/api.php` requests was 1. The only console message was a 404 for the browser's own `/favicon.ico` request.

### 2. P63-geleiding: de maanlander remt af

Verified by two builders. `vendor/bin/phpunit` passed with 62 tests and 2882 assertions, including `LandingGuidanceTest`, and `vendor/bin/phpstan analyse` reported no errors. The TTF solver converged in 4 iterations on the start state, and in at most 4 over 304 guidance passes. Through `/api.php` the DPS ignited at 10% throttle, went to 100% after 26 s and throttled back to 42% at t=500 s. At t=632 s the chapter ended with `outcome: "missed"` in P64 at 2305.6 m, with 14.6% of the fuel left. In Chrome, four screenshots showed the flame small at 10% throttle, long at 100% and shorter at 42% and 33%, while the fuel bar fell from full to 50%, 24% and 15%. The page showed "Outcome: missed".

- `src/Trace/Routine.php`: new enum with the cases this slice sets (P63LM, QUADGUID, ROOTPSRS, STARTP64, BURNBABY), each with label, file, line, url and `toArray`
- `src/Guidance/Luminary/LandingGuidance.php`: QUADGUID port with a Newton TTF solver after ROOTPSRS (at most 8 iterations), site frame, gravity compensation, thrust limit, pitch and thrust fraction
- `src/Guidance/Luminary/GuidanceCommand.php`: result value object of one guidance pass
- `src/Guidance/Luminary/Throttle.php`: DPS throttle logic (10-60% or 100%, with hysteresis)
- `src/Scenario/LandingTargets.php`: pad-loaded braking targets, chosen so P63 ends at about 2300 m, plus the TTF at which P63 switches to P64
- `src/Scenario/StartStates.php`: `LANDING_HORIZONTAL_SPEED` set to the plan's 1690 m/s
- `src/Agc/Agc.php`: added `?Routine $currentRoutine`
- `src/Chapter/Landing/LandingChapter.php`: DPS ignites at 10% on start, goes flat out after 26 s, QUADGUID runs every 2 s, switches to P64 and ends `missed`; optional start-state constructor argument for tests
- `src/Simulation/Simulation.php`: tick response carries `agc.routine` and the P63 and P64 program names
- `public/assets/views/landing.js`: flame scaled by throttle, fuel bar, throttle readout
- `public/index.php`: page text no longer says the lander starts without thrust
- `tests/Guidance/LandingGuidanceTest.php`: TTF convergence, P63 to P64 at 1800-2800 m, ignition then flat out at 26 s, throttle range and fuel
- `tests/Guidance/ThrottleTest.php`: throttle range and hysteresis
- `tests/Trace/RoutineTest.php`: routine fields and the QUADGUID url
- `tests/Chapter/LandingChapterTest.php`: adapted to the powered start state; crash at contact via free fall from 100 m
- `tests/Simulation/SimulationTest.php`: adapted; routine P63LM at start, `missed` at P64 between 1800 and 2800 m

### 3. De DSKY toont wat de computer doet

`vendor/bin/phpunit` passed with 89 tests and 2956 assertions, including `DisplayFormatterTest`, and `vendor/bin/phpstan analyse` reported no errors. In Chrome, after the start the DSKY showed PROG 63, VERB 06, NOUN 63, R1 `+55446` (1690 m/s is 5544.6 ft/s), R2 `+00000` and R3 `+50000` (15240 m is 50000 ft), and the registers updated every 2 s. The COMP ACTY lamp switched on at every guidance pass (t=28.1, 30.1, 32.1 s) for about 0.5 s and was off in between. The console showed no CSP violations and no JS errors.

- `src/Dsky/Dsky.php`: DSKY display model (port of NVSUB/GODSP and DSPMM) with `requestDisplay`, `supplyData`, `showMajorMode`, `setLight` and `toArray`
- `src/Dsky/NounTable.php`: per-computer noun table; LM has N09, 60, 63, 64 and 68, CM has N09, taken from `ASSEMBLY_AND_OPERATION_INFORMATION.agc`
- `src/Dsky/Noun.php`: value type for a noun (1 to 3 components)
- `src/Dsky/NounComponent.php`: value type pairing a unit with a format
- `src/Dsky/Unit.php`: unit enum; SI to ft, ft/s and NM conversion, applied only by `DisplayFormatter`
- `src/Dsky/Format.php`: enum of the register formats
- `src/Dsky/DisplayFormatter.php`: sign plus 5 digits, clamps at 99999 without an alarm, blank for NaN
- `src/Dsky/Light.php`: enum of the 13 lights; lights without a model stay off
- `src/Agc/Agc.php`: the AGC owns its `Dsky`
- `src/Chapter/Landing/LandingChapter.php`: shows V06N63 at start and refreshes it every 2 s, sets PROG to 63 then 64, sets COMP ACTY from the guidance passes
- `src/Simulation/Simulation.php`: tick response carries the real `dsky` object
- `public/assets/dsky.js`: HTML/CSS DSKY (display, 13 lights, 19 keys drawn but disabled), `textContent` only
- `public/assets/app.js`: creates the DSKY and renders it on every tick
- `public/index.php`: `.stage` wrapper and the DSKY section next to the canvas
- `public/assets/style.css`: DSKY layout and colours, a CSS flash animation, the stage layout
- `tests/Dsky/DisplayFormatterTest.php`: the plan's cases plus the time formats, NaN and unit conversion
- `tests/Dsky/NounTableTest.php`: N63 and N68 components; each computer knows only its own nouns
- `tests/Dsky/DskyTest.php`: V06N63 display, blank registers, noun change, flash, lights, refusal of an invalid verb, noun or value count
- `tests/Chapter/LandingChapterTest.php`: V06N63 at start, the 2 s refresh, COMP ACTY on and off, PROG 64 at hand-over
- `tests/Simulation/SimulationTest.php`: the response carries the real dsky shape
